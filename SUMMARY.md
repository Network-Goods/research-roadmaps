# Repository Summary: Research Roadmaps

## Overview

**research-roadmaps** is a documentation-focused repository by the [Network-Goods](https://github.com/Network-Goods) organization. Its mission is to optimize scientific coordination for the creation of research public goods with positive societal outcomes.

## What Are Research Roadmaps?

Research roadmaps are **goal-centered graph representations** of a strategy to achieve research milestones, enriched with quantitative data. They serve to:

- Architect new research initiatives (new hypotheses, problems, or experiments)
- Sketch value flows in research networks and highlight high-probability paths for impact
- Identify co-funders and research teams to drive impact
- Highlight and de-risk attachment points for bounties, Hypercerts, or retroactive funding

## Goals

The project aims to:

1. **Enable multi-group collaboration** — Allow multiple groups to interact via individual roadmaps and connect research/product roadmaps to converge on the best research paths
2. **Build a shared research network** — Create a shared network of research goals, milestones, and dependencies to bootstrap collaborations and align researchers with funders
3. **Bring open-source principles to research** — Promote immediate, continuous, and public sharing of research visions, goals, and dependencies so others can discover and build on them

## Philosophy

Roadmaps are designed to be **forked, adapted, and merged** — dynamic instruments that absorb signals from any stakeholder who wants to improve discoverability of science: who's making or funding what, why, and what comes next.

### Issue Template Structure (`epic.md`)

The Epic issue template follows an Agile structure with:
- **What** — Description of what will be accomplished
- **Why** — Connection to OKRs
- **How** — Primary tasks (≤5) with definitions of done
- **References** — Relevant documents and links
- **Hope-based metrics** — Gold and silver hopes for success criteria
